[PROJECT: RETAIL STOCKOUT RISK PREDICTION]
Rubina built a machine-learning classification project to identify products at risk of stocking out.

The project analyzed inventory levels, units sold, demand forecasts, pricing, promotions, product categories, weather, regional information, and seasonal signals. Rubina engineered rolling-window and time-based features and compared tree-based modeling approaches.

A major challenge was severe class imbalance because true stockout cases were rare. She used XGBoost, SMOTE, class weighting, confusion matrices, classification reports, and feature-importance analysis to improve detection of the minority class.

In the project experiments, stockout-class recall improved from approximately 0.2 percent to approximately 90 percent. The business purpose was to help prioritize high-risk products for replenishment while recognizing the tradeoff between missed stockouts and false alerts.

Rubina understands that overall accuracy can be misleading for rare-event prediction. She therefore focused on minority-class recall and would extend the project with stricter time-based validation, precision-recall threshold analysis, business-cost estimates for errors, model monitoring, and an operational dashboard.

GitHub repository: https://github.com/rubinaalmas/Retail-Stockout-Prediction
